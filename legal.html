Disclaimer

The information on this website is general guidance on matters that may interest you. It is not professional advice and should not be relied on as such. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Tan & Co (Birmingham) Ltd accepts no liability for any loss arising from use of this website or reliance on its content. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

Privacy

Tan & Co (Birmingham) Ltd is the data controller for personal information you give us through this website, by email or by phone.

What we collect

Your name, contact details and whatever you tell us in your enquiry. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to provide our services and meet our legal and professional obligations (including anti-money-laundering checks). We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your consent.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as the law and our professional rules require.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@tanandco.co.uk. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
