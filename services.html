Everything youraccounts need

Three areas of work, one team. Most clients use all three — we keep the books, file the returns and advise on what comes next.

01

Tax compliance& planning

Advice and returns for every part of UK tax — personal, corporate and VAT — with planning that stays within the rules and the deadlines.

  • Self Assessment registration and tax returns for individuals, the self-employed and partners
  • Corporation Tax returns (CT600) and company tax planning
  • Tax planning — using the allowances and reliefs you are entitled to
  • Tax appeals, and support through HMRC enquiries and investigations
  • VAT registration, quarterly VAT returns under Making Tax Digital, and deregistration

Good to know

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax started on 6 April 2026 for sole traders and landlords with income over £50,000. It reaches income over £30,000 from April 2027 and over £20,000 from April 2028 — quarterly updates through compatible software, then a final return. We can set you up and file for you.

02

Business start-ups& reorganisation

Whether you are opening your first shop or restructuring a family business, we explain the options and handle the paperwork.

  • Advice on the best structure for you: sole trader, partnership or limited company
  • Company formation and registration with Companies House and HMRC
  • Share transfers and changes of directors, company secretary and registered address
  • Reorganising, incorporating or winding down an existing business

Good to know

Companies House identity checks. Since 18 November 2025, every new company director and person with significant control must verify their identity. Existing directors verify when they file their next confirmation statement. We can guide you through it.

03

Accounts preparation& outsourcing

We prepare accounts for organisations of every size and complexity — from a single takeaway to a group of companies, and charities too.

  • Bookkeeping, and business plans and forecasts for bank loans
  • Year-end accounts for sole traders, partnerships and limited companies
  • Management accounts, so you know where the business stands during the year
  • Payroll and PAYE: payslips, Real Time Information returns to HMRC, P60s, P45s and P11Ds
  • Accounts for charities and not-for-profit organisations

Good to know

Limited company deadlines run from your year end: accounts to Companies House within 9 months, Corporation Tax 9 months and a day, and the CT600 within 12 months. Try the calculator below.
