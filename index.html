Who we are

The West Midlands’first firm for theChinese community

Philip Tan opened Tan & Co in 1975 to give Chinese families and businesses in Birmingham an accountant who understood them. Half a century on, the firm has built its name on experience and a consistently high standard of client service.

Most of our clients are businesses — limited companies, partnerships and sole traders — alongside individuals and charities. Many have been with us for decades.

We work with

  • Limited companies
  • Sole traders & partnerships
  • Restaurants, takeaways & retail
  • Individuals & landlords
  • Charities & not-for-profits
